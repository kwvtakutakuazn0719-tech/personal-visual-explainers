How to Measure Your Foot

  1. 1 Stand on a flat surface with your heel against a wall.
  2. 2 Mark the tip of your longest toe and measure the distance to the wall in mm.
  3. 3 Measure both feet and use the larger measurement. Feet are often different sizes.
  4. 4 Match your measurement to the Foot Length column above. If between sizes, go up.
